# Online Primary-Text & Book Resources

## Sanskrit / Indic Text Repositories

- [Muktabodha Digital Library](https://muktabodha-digital-library.org/welcome) — Primary Sanskrit digital library; especially important for Śaiva, Śākta, Vīraśaiva, Pāñcarātra, Śrīvidyā, Nātha and Yoga materials.
- [Sanskrit Library](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/) — Digital Sanskrit texts and analytical tools.
- [GRETIL](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html) — Downloadable Sanskrit, Pāli and Prākrit e-texts.
- [SARIT](https://sarit.indology.info/apps/sarit-pm/docs/welcome.html) — Documented and machine-readable Indic editions in XML, EPUB and PDF.
- [INDOLOGY Virtual E-Text Archive](https://indology.info/virtual-e-text-archive-of-indic-texts/) — Discovery index for Indic e-text repositories.
- [Gītā Supersite — IIT Kanpur](https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/) — Sanskrit texts, translations and commentarial material.
- [TITUS](https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/texte2.htm) — Sanskrit, Indo-European and related textual resources.

## Manuscript / Book Collections

- [University of Tokyo Sanskrit Manuscripts](https://da.dl.itc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/portal/en/collection/skt_manuscripts) — Digital surrogates of Sanskrit manuscripts.
- [Hill Museum & Manuscript Library](https://hmml.org/) — Large-scale manuscript digitization and archival resources.
- [Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute](https://bori.ac.in/) — Major Indian manuscript and Oriental research institution.
- [National Mission for Manuscripts](https://www.namami.gov.in/) — Indian manuscript cataloguing, conservation and digitization.
- [IGNCA Manuscripts](https://ignca.gov.in/online-digital-resources/manuscripts/) — Manuscript catalogues and digital resources.

## Note

The local `books/` collection remains the primary physical/digital holding. These links are external discovery sources and should not replace the local books.
