INDIAN PHILOSOPHY
RESEARCH PAPERS
================

Purpose
-------

This folder contains research-paper discovery resources,
scholarly repositories, thesis repositories, digital-text
infrastructures, open-access research and selected individual
research papers relevant to Indian philosophy.

Research should be classified by the philosophical problem
actually investigated.

Major research domains include:

Nyaya
Vaisheshika
Mimamsa
Vedanta
Samkhya
Yoga
Buddhist philosophy
Jain philosophy
Carvaka / Lokayata
Indian logic
Indian epistemology
Indian metaphysics
Indian philosophy of language
Indian philosophy of mind
Philosophy of grammar
Aesthetics
Ethics
Comparative philosophy
History of Indian philosophy
Sanskrit philosophical literature

Principle
---------

Do not treat a database as evidence merely because it indexes
a resource.

Trace important claims to:

1. Primary text
2. Critical edition
3. Manuscript
4. Scholarly translation
5. Research paper
6. Dissertation
7. Scholarly commentary

Original languages should be preserved whenever possible.

Relevant languages may include:

Sanskrit
Pali
Prakrit
Tibetan
Tamil
Telugu
Kannada
Malayalam
Bengali
Hindi
Marathi
Gujarati
and other Indian languages.

Comparative research must involve an actual comparison of
arguments, concepts, methods or philosophical problems.

Do not classify a work as philosophical merely because a
database labels it "philosophy."

Evaluate the actual method, argument, assumptions, evidence,
and conclusions.
