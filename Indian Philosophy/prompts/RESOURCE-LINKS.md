## Prompt design rule

Prompts should explicitly distinguish:

1. Primary text
2. Traditional commentary
3. Translation
4. Critical edition
5. Modern scholarship
6. Institutional resource
7. Popular/general material
8. Colonial/Orientalist interpretation
9. Traditional/non-Western interpretation

The uploaded `deep-research-report.md` is the baseline research document for this collection.
