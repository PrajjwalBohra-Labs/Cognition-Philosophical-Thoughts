# Curriculum & Learning Resources

## Indian Philosophy

- [Sanskrit Library](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/) — Sanskrit learning and research tools.
- [Gītā Supersite — IIT Kanpur](https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/) — Textual and commentary-based study.
- [ICPR](https://icpr.in/) — Indian philosophical research, programmes and academic activities.
- [ICPR Journals](https://icpr.in/journals/) — Research-oriented philosophical journal resource.
- [ICPR Publications](https://icpr.in/publication/) — Classical Indian philosophy, contemporary philosophy, readings, references and seminar proceedings.
- [SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/) — Buddhist canonical study and translations.
- [Jain eLibrary](https://www.jainelibrary.org/) — Jain textual resources.

## Recommended use

Use these sources to construct reading sequences, primary-text paths, Sanskrit study paths, and advanced research curricula.
