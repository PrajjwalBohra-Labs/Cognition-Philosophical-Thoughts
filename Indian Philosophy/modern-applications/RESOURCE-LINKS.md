# Modern Applications, AI & Digital Humanities

## Indian Philosophy + Computation

- [SARIT](https://sarit.indology.info/apps/sarit-pm/docs/welcome.html) — TEI/XML corpus with search, retrieval and downloadable machine-readable editions.
- [Sanskrit Library](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/) — Digital Sanskrit research tools.
- [TITUS](https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/texte2.htm) — Text search and linguistic resources.
- [SanskritShala](https://cnerg.iitkgp.ac.in/sanskritshala) — Sanskrit NLP toolkit for segmentation, morphology, parsing and annotation.
- [IndicIRSuite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09508) — Multilingual information retrieval research for Indian languages.
- [Digital Library Initiatives in India](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13594) — Survey of Indian digital-library initiatives, metadata and access issues.

## AI + Indian Philosophy

- [Pramāṇa: Fine-Tuning LLMs for Epistemic Reasoning through Navya-Nyāya](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04937) — AI/LLM research using Navya-Nyāya epistemic reasoning.
- [Cubical Type Theoretic Navya-Nyāya](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12548) — Formalization connecting Navya-Nyāya with type-theoretic methods.
- [Darshana Graph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18222) — Computational corpus and comparative study of Indian philosophical texts/commentaries.
