# Dictionaries, Lexicons & Terminology

- [Sanskrit Library — Cologne Dictionaries](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/cologne.html) — Major digital Sanskrit lexicographical resources.
- [Sanskrit Library](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/) — Sanskrit research tools.
- [TITUS](https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/texte2.htm) — Linguistic/textual resources.
- [SARIT](https://sarit.indology.info/apps/sarit-pm/docs/welcome.html) — Searchable Indic corpus for contextual terminology research.

## Priority terminology areas

- Sanskrit philosophical vocabulary
- Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika terminology
- Navya-Nyāya technical vocabulary
- Mīmāṃsā terminology
- Bhartṛhari / Vyākaraṇa terminology
- Buddhist pramāṇa vocabulary
- Jain philosophical terminology
- Pāli
- Prākrit
- Tibetan philosophical terminology

Do not treat an English translation as automatically equivalent to the Sanskrit technical term.
