# Indian Philosophy — Article Resources

- [Journal of Indian Philosophy](https://link.springer.com/journal/10781) — Primary discovery point for scholarly articles.
- [Smoke and Fire: Sign Inference in Greek and in Indian Epistemology](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10781-024-09572-w) — Comparative Greek/Indian epistemology.
- [Two Uses of Anekāntavāda](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10781-024-09577-5) — Jain philosophy.
- [From Anekānta-vāda to Sarva-tantra-sva-tantra](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10781-024-09570-y) — Jain philosophical pluralism.
- [Dharmamegha in Yoga and Yogācāra](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10781-020-09432-3) — Yoga/Yogācāra.
- [Jayarāśi Bhaṭṭa: A Sceptic or Materialist?](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10781-020-09431-4) — Indian materialism and scepticism.
- [Advayavajra's Nairātmyāprakāśa: A Critical Edition](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10781-025-09611-0) — Buddhist textual scholarship.
- [From Anekānta-vāda to Sarva-tantra-sva-tantra](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10781-024-09570-y) — Pluralism about philosophical systems.
