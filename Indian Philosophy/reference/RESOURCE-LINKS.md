# Master Reference & Index Resources

- [Muktabodha Digital Library](https://muktabodha-digital-library.org/welcome) — Primary Sanskrit digital library.
- [GRETIL](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html) — Indic e-text register.
- [SARIT](https://sarit.indology.info/apps/sarit-pm/docs/welcome.html) — Machine-readable Indic corpus.
- [TITUS](https://titus.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/texte2.htm) — Textual and linguistic database.
- [IGNCA Manuscripts](https://ignca.gov.in/online-digital-resources/manuscripts/) — Manuscript catalogues.
- [National Mission for Manuscripts](https://www.namami.gov.in/) — Indian national manuscript programme.
- [University of Tokyo Sanskrit Manuscripts](https://da.dl.itc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/portal/en/collection/skt_manuscripts) — Sanskrit manuscript database.
- [HMML](https://hmml.org/) — Manuscript archive.
- [BORI](https://bori.ac.in/) — Indian manuscript/research institution.
- [ICPR](https://icpr.in/) — Indian Council of Philosophical Research.
- [JICPR](https://icpr.in/journals/) — Journal of Indian Council of Philosophical Research.
- [Journal of Indian Philosophy](https://link.springer.com/journal/10781) — Major scholarly journal.
- [SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/) — Buddhist textual resource.
- [Jain eLibrary](https://www.jainelibrary.org/) — Jain textual resource.
- [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/) — Philosophy bibliography.

## Function

This folder should contain indexes, bibliographies, timelines, source maps, terminology maps and research guides — not duplicate copies of books.
