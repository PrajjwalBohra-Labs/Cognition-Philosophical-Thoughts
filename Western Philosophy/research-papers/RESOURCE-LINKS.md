# Western Philosophy — Research Databases

## Initial Resource Set

- [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/) — Core philosophy bibliography.
- [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/) — Scholarly journals and books.
- [Project MUSE](https://muse.jhu.edu/) — Humanities and social-science research.
- [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/) — Open-access journal directory.
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) — Broad scholarly discovery.
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/) — Open research archive, especially useful for logic, AI and computational philosophy.
