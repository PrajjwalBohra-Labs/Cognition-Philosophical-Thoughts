# Western Philosophy — Books, Primary Texts & Other Works

## Classical

- [Perseus Digital Library](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/) — Greek and Roman primary texts and scholarship.
- [Internet Classics Archive — MIT](https://classics.mit.edu/) — Classical Greek/Roman works, mainly in English translation.

## Early Modern / Modern

- [Early Modern Texts](https://www.earlymoderntexts.com/) — Accessible editions of major early-modern philosophical works.
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) — Large digital library.
- [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/) — Bibliographic and digitized-book discovery.

## Important

This is only the initial Western resource set. A separate comprehensive Western Philosophy research project should later add original-language corpora, critical editions, manuscript archives, philosophical societies, specialist databases and global scholarship.
