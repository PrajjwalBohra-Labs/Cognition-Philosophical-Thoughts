# Western Philosophy — Article & Reference Resources

## Initial Resource Set

A dedicated deep research pass for Western Philosophy is still to be conducted. These are the core discovery resources already identified.

- [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/) — Scholarly philosophy reference work.
- [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/) — Open-access philosophy encyclopedia.
- [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/) — Philosophy bibliography and article discovery.
- [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/) — Scholarly journals and books.
- [Project MUSE](https://muse.jhu.edu/) — Humanities and social-science scholarship.
- [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/) — Open-access journal directory.

## Important

Do not treat SEP/IEP as primary sources. Use them as secondary/reference material and trace claims back to primary texts and scholarly literature.
