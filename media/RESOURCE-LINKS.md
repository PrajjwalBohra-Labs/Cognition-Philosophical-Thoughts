# Lectures, Audio, Video & Media

## Institutional / Traditional Sources

- [ICPR](https://icpr.in/) — Indian philosophical programmes, workshops and academic activities.
- [National Mission for Manuscripts](https://www.namami.gov.in/) — Manuscript studies, digitization, conservation and educational material.
- [Muktabodha Digital Library](https://muktabodha.org/digital-library/) — Sanskrit textual and contextual material.
- [SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/) — Buddhist textual/audio-learning ecosystem.
- [National Mission for Manuscripts — Introduction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6WUOEx5ZdA) — Introduction to manuscript preservation, digitization and archival work.
