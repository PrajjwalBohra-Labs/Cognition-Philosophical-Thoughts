# Philosophers

This file provides a working reference to the principal philosophers and authors represented by the repository.

Historical dates are approximate where exact dating is disputed. Do not treat approximate chronology as exact biographical fact.

---

# 1. Gautama / Akṣapāda

**Tradition:** Nyāya

**Principal work represented:** *Nyāya Sūtras*

## Philosophical role

Foundational Nyāya framework concerning:

- knowledge;
- means of knowledge;
- inference;
- debate;
- reasoning;
- error;
- self;
- liberation;
- philosophical methodology.

## Study significance

Begin here for the classical architecture of Nyāya, while using *Tarkasaṅgraha* as a compact conceptual guide.

---

# 2. Kaṇāda

**Tradition:** Vaiśeṣika

**Principal work represented:** *Vaiśeṣika Sūtras*

## Philosophical role

Foundational analysis of:

- substances;
- qualities;
- action;
- universals;
- particularity;
- inherence;
- absence;
- causation;
- self;
- mind;
- time;
- space.

## Study significance

Essential for understanding the ontological side of the later Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika synthesis.

---

# 3. Annambhaṭṭa

**Tradition:** Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika

**Principal work represented:** *Tarkasaṅgraha*

## Philosophical role

Systematic introductory presentation of major Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika categories and epistemological concepts.

## Study significance

Useful as an entry point and conceptual reference while reading more difficult texts.

---

# 4. Jayanta Bhaṭṭa

**Tradition:** Nyāya

**Principal work represented:** *Nyāyamañjarī*

## Philosophical role

Broad philosophical analysis involving:

- epistemology;
- metaphysics;
- rival schools;
- objections and replies;
- argumentation.

## Study significance

Particularly useful for seeing Nyāya operating within a larger intellectual debate rather than as an isolated list of doctrines.

---

# 5. Gaṅgeśa Upādhyāya

**Tradition:** Navya-Nyāya

**Principal work represented:** *Tattvacintāmaṇi*

## Philosophical role

Advanced technical analysis of:

- perception;
- inference;
- comparison;
- testimony;
- definitions;
- relations;
- epistemic structure.

## Study significance

A major transition from classical Nyāya exposition to highly technical analytical philosophy.

---

# 6. Jaimini

**Tradition:** Pūrva Mīmāṃsā

**Principal work represented:** *Pūrva Mīmāṃsā Sūtra*

## Philosophical role

Central concerns include:

- dharma;
- Vedic authority;
- ritual action;
- interpretation;
- verbal authority;
- sentence meaning.

## Study significance

Provides an essential rival framework for comparison with Nyāya on language, knowledge, authority, and interpretation.

---

# 7. Bhartṛhari

**Tradition:** Vyākaraṇa / philosophy of language

**Principal work represented:** *Vākyapadīya*

## Philosophical role

Major themes:

- *śabda*;
- sentence;
- word;
- meaning;
- cognition;
- *sphoṭa*;
- relation between linguistic expression and thought.

## Study significance

Essential for the repository's philosophy-of-language component.

# 9. Author vs Tradition

Do not assume:

```text
one author = entire school
```

A school develops through:

```text
Foundational text
 ↓
Commentaries
 ↓
Objections
 ↓
Reformulations
 ↓
Technical developments
```

Therefore, distinguish:

- author's formulation;
- later school interpretation;
- commentator's reconstruction;
- modern scholarly interpretation.
